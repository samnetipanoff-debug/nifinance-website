04 / What the system needs

Intelligence needs foundations.

A language model alone cannot provide reliable business understanding without the context around the numbers.

A / DATA CONTEXT

Can we trust and compare it?

Consistent definitions, clean business entities, traceable sources and aligned reporting periods.

B / BUSINESS LOGIC

What actually drives the outcome?

Unit economics, customer lifetime value, retention, pricing, variable costs and working capital.

C / GOVERNANCE

Who decides — and on what basis?

Visible assumptions, confidence levels, risk limits, auditability and human approval.
